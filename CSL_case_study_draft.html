The Project

True Crime Podcast — "Shadow Evidence"

A production company needed a complete sonic identity for their new true crime series: theme music, episode intros, segment transitions, and ad bumpers.

The Brief

Dark, cinematic, but not melodramatic. The music needed to build tension without telegraphing the narrative beats. Minimalist orchestral with electronic textures.

The Approach

We built three core motifs: a main theme (piano + strings), a suspense variant (synth pads + sub-bass), and a resolution tag (warm ambient). Each episode got a custom intro/outro pairing.

The Delivery

All 12 episodes delivered in full within 10 days of brief approval. Mastered WAV + MP3, broadcast-ready, with full buyout licensing.

"We'd been burned by composers who overpromised and underdelivered. Fresh Water was the opposite — honest about timelines, transparent about process, and the music spoke for itself. The client feedback on the theme alone made the entire series worth it."

— Marcus Chen, Executive Producer, Nightfall Media
