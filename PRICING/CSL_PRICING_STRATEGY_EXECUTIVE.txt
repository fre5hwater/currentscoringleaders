      CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — PRICING STRATEGY (EXECUTIVE VERSION)
        Internal quoting framework for premium custom scoring services
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE

This document defines the internal pricing and quoting framework for Current
Scoring Leaders.

It is designed to support:
- consistent pricing decisions
- premium market positioning
- clearer scope control
- healthier margins
- alignment between outreach, website messaging, calls, and formal quotes

This is an internal strategy document.
It is not intended to be published externally in full.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. IDEAL CLIENT PROFILE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRIMARY PRIORITY CLIENTS

1. Video creators with strong editing and weak sonic identity
2. Podcasters building a serious show brand
3. Indie game teams with worldbuilding-heavy projects
4. Production houses and agencies needing dependable custom scoring support

BEST-FIT CLIENT TRAITS

- clear brand or narrative intent
- visible quality standards
- repeat-project potential or referral value
- dissatisfaction with generic stock music outcomes
- willingness to pay for specificity, speed, and reliability

LOWER-PRIORITY CLIENT TRAITS

- unclear scope
- unrealistic timelines
- price-only buyers
- excessive revision risk
- broad ownership demands without budget support

3. QUOTING PRINCIPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pricing should reflect:
- strategic value to the client
- scope complexity
- delivery burden
- usage rights
- stakeholder load
- timeline pressure
- portfolio or relationship value

Pricing should not be determined only by runtime.
A short asset with high brand value can justify a higher fee than a longer,
lower-stakes deliverable.

Use these bands as a quoting framework, not a fixed rate card.
Maintain consistency, but preserve judgment.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. CORE PRICING VARIABLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every quote should consider the following variables:

- scope
- runtime
- cue count
- revision rounds
- deliverable count
- stems or alternate versions
- timeline
- collaboration load
- licensing / usage rights
- term length
- distribution size
- exclusivity level
- ownership / buyout terms
- long-term relationship value

USAGE RIGHTS GUIDANCE

Lower-priced work usually aligns with narrower usage rights.
Higher-priced work should be considered when the client requests:
- perpetual use
- broad commercial distribution
- paid advertising use
- exclusive rights
- full work-for-hire transfer or buyout

5. PRICING BANDS BY OFFER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 SIGNATURE THEME PACKAGE

Range: $600–2,500+

Entry band: $600–900
Typical fit:
- solo creator or early-stage show
- limited usage footprint
- one main use case
- low revision complexity

Typical scope:
- one main theme
- intro cut
- outro cut
- one revision round

Standard band: $900–1,500
Typical fit:
- established brand or creator
- multiple deliverables
- moderate collaboration
- stronger branding expectations

Typical scope:
- main theme
- intro/outro
- bumper or stinger variation
- two revision rounds
- multiple export versions

Premium band: $1,500–2,500+
Typical fit:
- brand-critical launch or rebrand
- expanded usage rights
- elevated production expectations
- multiple stakeholders

Typical scope:
- multi-version theme system
- stronger polish and arrangement depth
- broader deliverable set
- additional cutdowns or branded variants


5.2 VIDEO / EPISODE SCORING

Range: $600–5,000+

Short-form band: $600–1,200
Typical fit:
- one video, trailer, or short branded piece
- approximately 30 to 90 seconds of scoring
- limited cue count
- straightforward revision path

Mid-range band: $1,200–2,500
Typical fit:
- long-form creator video, documentary section, or branded content piece
- multiple cues or longer runtime
- stronger scene-to-scene musical intent
- moderate collaboration load

Premium band: $2,500–5,000+
Typical fit:
- complex narrative work
- multiple scenes or cue families
- significant editorial interaction
- higher usage value or wider distribution

Boundary guidance:
- move upward when cue count increases materially
- move upward when stems, alternates, or sync precision increase
- move upward when rights expand beyond limited project use


5.3 PODCAST BRANDING PACKAGES

Range: $300–1,800+

Entry band: $300–600
Typical fit:
- independent show
- intro and outro only
- straightforward brand direction

Standard band: $600–1,000
Typical fit:
- growing show or network property
- intro, outro, bumper, and alternate cuts
- stronger brand alignment expectations

Premium band: $1,000–1,800+
Typical fit:
- rebrand, network launch, or higher-visibility show
- multiple branded variants
- broader use across episodes, promos, or show formats

Refresh retainer: $150–500/month
Use only when scope is clearly limited and defined in advance.


5.4 GAME / NARRATIVE PACKS

Range: $800–7,500+

Small pack: $800–1,500
Typical fit:
- smaller indie title
- limited cue set
- menu, ambient, and one or two event cues

Mid pack: $1,500–3,500
Typical fit:
- stronger indie release
- several cues or themes
- moderate immersion and worldbuilding requirements

Large pack: $3,500–7,500+
Typical fit:
- narrative-heavy title
- multiple themes, gameplay states, or environments
- milestone-based delivery and heavier coordination

Boundary guidance:
- move upward with cue count, adaptive needs, implementation support,
  or expanded rights expectations


5.5 RETAINERS

Creator retainer: $1,000–2,000/month
Best fit:
- active creators with recurring releases
- repeatable custom music needs
- limited number of monthly deliverables

Studio / agency retainer: $2,000–5,000+/month
Best fit:
- recurring production environments
- multiple projects under one operating relationship
- need for faster responsiveness and dependable scheduling

Retainers should never be sold without explicit scope controls.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. RETAINER GUARDRAILS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every retainer should define:
- number of deliverables per month
- target response window
- revision limits
- included call / review time
- rollover policy
- overage pricing
- rush treatment rules
- rights granted within monthly fee

Recommended default retainer posture:
- no unlimited revisions
- no unlimited deliverables
- no undefined priority access
- no silent expansion of rights or scope

If scope is likely to vary heavily month to month,
quote a hybrid model: base retainer plus overage pricing.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. PRICE MOVERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Increase quote when there is:
- rush delivery
- high revision complexity
- multiple stakeholders
- large number of alternates or exports
- stem delivery requirements
- exclusivity demands
- wide commercial usage
- perpetual use or buyout requests
- high collaboration burden
- uncertain scope

Reduce quote selectively when there is:
- strong portfolio value
- strong referral leverage
- strong long-term client potential
- recurring project potential
- unusually simple scope with limited revision risk

Any price reduction should be strategic,
not reactive.

8. POSITIONING RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CSL pricing communication should reinforce:
- custom work
- premium perception
- strategic clarity
- professionalism
- confidence without rigidity

Preferred pricing language:
- starting at
- typical range
- quoted by scope
- based on usage and deliverables
- custom proposal available

Avoid price positioning that undermines premium perception.
Maintain consistency in how price is explained across website, outreach,
and calls.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9. SALES PATH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Recommended conversion path:
- website or outbound outreach
- discovery conversation
- scoped follow-up
- quote or proposal
- close
- delivery
- upsell or retainer discussion

What pricing must do at each stage:

Website:
- qualify lightly
- support premium perception
- prevent low-fit bargain inquiries

Outreach:
- mention only the most relevant range
- keep focus on value and fit
- avoid overwhelming details

Discovery call:
- confirm scope, rights, timeline, and decision-making structure
- identify where the project sits within the pricing framework

Proposal:
- show final scope, rights, timeline, deliverables, revisions, and price clearly

11. INITIAL CAPACITY MODEL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This section is a starting operating assumption and should be refined with real
tracking after the first 30 to 60 days of delivery.

Initial capacity guardrails:
- up to 4 theme-package projects per month, or
- 2 mid-sized scoring projects per month, or
- 1 larger scoring package plus 1 to 2 smaller package projects

Do not sell retainers aggressively until:
- delivery timing is being tracked consistently
- revision load is understood
- actual average project hours are known

Capacity should be reviewed against:
- turnaround promises
- revision burden
- admin time
- outreach time
- mix / export time
- client communication time

The objective is not maximum volume.
The objective is controlled quality, profitable delivery, and repeatable client
experience.
