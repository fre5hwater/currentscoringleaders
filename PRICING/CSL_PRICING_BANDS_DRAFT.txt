IMPORTANT

These are internal pricing bands for quoting and positioning.
They should guide offers, not lock the business into rigid pricing.
Final quote should always consider:
- Scope
- timeline
- revisions
- exclusivity
- deliverables
- client budget
- long-term value of relationship

Use these bands as structure, not handcuffs.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. ADD-ONS / PRICE MOVERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Increase quote when there is:
- rush timeline
- heavy revision demand
- multiple deliverable versions
- stem exports
- exclusivity requirements
- complex sound design expectations
- frequent calls / collaboration load
- vague scope with high uncertainty

Lower quote strategically when there is:
- strong portfolio value
- long-term client potential
- referral leverage
- recurring work opportunity
- simple scope with low revision risk


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. POSITIONING RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Do NOT lead with cheapness.
Lead with:
- uniqueness
- identity
- professionalism
- clarity
- results

Use pricing language like:
- starts at
- typical range
- depends on scope
- quote based on project needs

Avoid looking random.
Avoid looking bargain-bin.
Avoid looking like a library replacement.

CSL should feel:
- premium
- direct
- custom
- practical
- flexible


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. HOW TO USE ON WEBSITE VS OUTREACH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEBSITE
- Use "starting at" or "custom quote" language
- Keep premium perception intact
- Avoid overwhelming with every internal band

OUTREACH
- Mention only relevant range for the prospect segment
- Match scope to their likely budget reality
- Use pricing to qualify, not scare everyone away

CALLS / QUOTES
- Use this sheet to anchor the conversation
- Shift up or down based on fit and scope
