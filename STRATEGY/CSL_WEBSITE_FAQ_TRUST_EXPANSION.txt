FAQ SECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. WHY CHOOSE CUSTOM SCORING OVER STOCK MUSIC?

Stock music solves convenience.
Custom scoring solves identity.

If your project needs to sound distinct, emotionally precise, or built around a
specific world, brand, or story, custom scoring gives you something stock
libraries cannot: music shaped around your actual creative goal.


2. DO YOU USE AI IN THE PROCESS?

Current Scoring Leaders is human-directed and project-specific.
Modern tools may be used where helpful in the workflow, but the musical
creative direction, composition choices, and project decisions stay intentional
and tailored to the client.

The goal is not to generate filler.
The goal is to create music with identity.


3. WHAT RIGHTS DO I RECEIVE?

Rights are defined clearly per project.
Depending on the agreement, projects may be structured as:
- work-for-hire
- exclusive license
- non-exclusive license

That means the rights package can match the actual use case instead of forcing
one rigid model onto every client.
If full ownership is needed, that can be addressed in the agreement.


4. HOW MANY REVISIONS ARE INCLUDED?

Revision scope depends on the package and project agreement.
In general, revisions are included and defined up front so the process stays
clear.

Smaller projects usually include a set number of revision rounds.
Larger projects may be handled through milestone feedback.
Major direction changes or re-composition requests are handled as scope changes,
not hidden surprises.


5. WHAT FILE FORMATS DO YOU DELIVER?

Final delivery typically includes:
- WAV
- MP3

Depending on the project, stems, multitracks, or MIDI can also be included or
added by agreement.
Deliverables are defined in writing before work begins.


6. DO YOU OFFER RUSH WORK?

Yes, when schedule and scope allow.
Rush delivery is handled by agreement and may include an added fee depending on
the timeline and project demands.

If timing is critical, include that in the initial inquiry so feasibility can be
assessed immediately.


7. DO YOU OFFER RECURRING PACKAGES OR RETAINERS?

Yes.
Current Scoring Leaders offers retainer-style support for creators, studios,
agencies, and recurring projects that need dependable custom music over time.

This is best for teams that want priority support and consistent scoring without
starting from zero every month.


8. DO YOU WORK WITH AGENCIES OR REFERRAL PARTNERS?

Yes.
Current Scoring Leaders can work directly with agencies, production houses,
editors, and creative partners who need a reliable custom music resource for
client projects.

Referral relationships are fit-driven, straightforward, and designed to make the
partner look stronger when custom music needs come up.


9. HOW DOES PRICING WORK?

There are clear public starting points for common project types, but final
pricing depends on scope, complexity, rights, timeline, and deliverables.

That keeps pricing honest without pretending every project fits into the same
box.

If you reach out with your project details, you will get a direct recommendation
on the best starting point.


10. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I INQUIRE?

You send the project, timeline, and goals.
If it looks like a fit, Current Scoring Leaders replies with a clear next step,
which may be a short discovery call, a written scope, or a direct quote.

The process is meant to be professional and simple, not a sales maze.

TRUST-BOOSTER COPY BLOCKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ORIGINALITY BLOCK

Every project is built for the client it is for.
No stock substitutions.
No recycled catalog filler presented as custom work.


2. RIGHTS CLARITY BLOCK

Usage rights are defined in writing before delivery.
That means fewer surprises, cleaner approvals, and clearer long-term use.


3. PROCESS CLARITY BLOCK

The process is structured around discovery, direction, drafts, revisions, and
final delivery.
Clients know what happens next and what is included.


4. PAYMENT CLARITY BLOCK

Projects typically begin with a 50% deposit and conclude with the remaining 50%
upon final delivery, unless a milestone structure is agreed in advance.
Final deliverables are released after full payment.


5. RESPONSE / REVIEW EXPECTATION BLOCK

Clients are given a defined review window so momentum does not disappear into
endless back-and-forth.
That keeps projects moving and expectations clear on both sides.


6. PROFESSIONALISM BLOCK

Current Scoring Leaders is structured to work like a real service business:
clear agreements, clear scope, clear delivery, and direct communication.


7. PORTFOLIO HONESTY BLOCK

If demo concepts are shown, they should be labeled honestly.
The goal is credibility, not pretending speculative work was paid client work.


8. FIT-BASED CTA BLOCK

If your project needs music with its own identity, reach out.
If it is a fit, you will get a direct next step, not an automated runaround.
