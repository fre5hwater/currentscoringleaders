SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BUSINESS CONCEPT:

Premium custom music composition service targeting creators, production companies,
and media studios. Differentiation: high-touch custom work + personalized relationships
vs. platform-based subscription catalogs.

MARKET OPPORTUNITY:

• Total Addressable Market (TAM): $5-10 billion (global music composition/licensing)
• Serviceable Market (SAM): $200-500 million (custom composition for creators)
• Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): $5-50 million (Year 1-5 realistic capture)

KEY INSIGHTS FROM ANALYSIS:

1. Competitors (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, AudioJungle) are platform/catalog-based
   - NOT in custom composition business
   - Create market gap for premium custom service
   - Revenue: $50-120M each (growing 40-50% annually)

2. Individual composer economics are favorable
   - Median income: $63,400 (2020 BLS data)
   - Specialists earn: $100,000-300,000+ annually
   - Passive income potential: 20-40% of revenue (from licensing)

3. Market has clear buyer personas:
   - YouTube creators (200K+ targets)
   - Production companies (30K+ targets)
   - Podcasters (100K+ targets)
   - Gaming studios (5K+ targets)

4. Referral model is highly scalable:
   - 20-30 active referral partners can generate $50K-100K/year recurring
   - Low CAC (customer acquisition cost) from referrals
   - High retention (satisfied referral partners)

BUSINESS MODEL:

Revenue Streams:
  1. Direct composition work (50-60% of revenue): $600-50,000 per project
  2. Licensing deals (30-40% of revenue): $500-5,000 per license
  3. Retainer clients (10-20% of revenue): $1,000-5,000/month
  4. Passive catalog income (5-10%, grows over time): $50-1,000/month

Target Customer Mix:
  • Direct buyers: 60% of revenue (high-touch, higher price)
  • Referral partners: 40% of revenue (volume, recurring)

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING:

vs. Epidemic Sound/Artlist/AudioJungle:
  ✓ Custom work (they don't do)
  ✓ Personal relationships (they're algorithmic)
  ✓ Faster turnaround (48-96 hours vs weeks)
  ✓ Higher-touch service (vs browsing catalogs)
  ✓ Specialization (niche expertise)
  ✗ Larger catalog (can't compete on volume)
  ✗ Lower price (won't compete on cheap)

Year 2: $265,000 revenue, $145,750 net profit
Year 3: $520,000 revenue, $312,000 net profit
Year 5: $760,000+ revenue, $456,000+ net profit

STATUS: ✓ COMPLETE - See separate document "MUSIC_SCORING_LEGAL_FRAMEWORK.txt"

SUMMARY:

Business Structure:
  → Recommended: LLC (Limited Liability Company)
  → Liability protection + pass-through taxation
  → Cost: ~$100-300 to form (varies by state)

IP Protection:
  → Copyright all original compositions with US Copyright Office ($65/registration)
  → Trademark business name/logo ($250-400)
  → Register with PRO (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC) for performance royalties
  → Register with SoundExchange for digital royalties

Contracts:
  → Master Service Agreement (MSA): For recurring clients
  → Work-for-Hire Agreement: For one-off projects
  → Licensing Agreement: For licensing revenue models

Insurance Requirements:
  → General Liability: $1M minimum (~$400-800/year)
  → Errors & Omissions: $1M (~$500-1,000/year)
  → Total insurance: ~$1,200-1,800/year

Compliance Calendar:
  → Quarterly: Estimated tax payments (April 15, June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15)
  → Annually: Tax returns (April 15 deadline)
  → PRO registration and annual royalty tracking
  → Sales tax nexus determination (if applicable)

RISK MITIGATION:
  ✓ Documented creation process (protects from infringement claims)
  ✓ Licensed materials tracking (no unauthorized samples)
  ✓ Client confidentiality agreements (NDA templates ready)
  ✓ Data security protocols (encrypt file transfers)
  ✓ Incident response plan (if breach occurs)

COMPETITORS ANALYZED:

1. EPIDEMIC SOUND (Subscription Music Platform)
   • Est. 2023 Revenue: $50-80M
   • Est. 2024 Revenue: $70-110M (40-50% growth)
   • Subscribers: 3-4M paying users
   • Strength: Brand recognition, scale, profitability
   • Weakness: No custom work, curated only, creator unhappy with splits
   • Market Gap: Custom composition + high-touch service

2. ARTLIST (Premium Subscription Music + SFX + Templates)
   • Est. 2023 Revenue: $80-120M
   • Est. 2024 Revenue: $120-180M (50-70% growth)
   • Subscribers: 4-5M paying users
   • Strength: Premium positioning, ecosystem approach
   • Weakness: High churn, aggressive CAC, no custom work
   • Market Gap: Personal relationships + custom composition

3. AUDIOJUNGLE (Marketplace Model)
   • Est. 2023 Revenue: $60-100M (AudioJungle division of Envato)
   • Est. 2024 Revenue: $70-120M (15-25% growth)
   • Sellers: 50K+ active creators
   • Strength: Scale, diverse catalog, established brand
   • Weakness: Creator dissatisfaction (low payouts), race to bottom
   • Market Gap: Higher creator payouts + custom work

KEY FINDING: None of the three major competitors offer custom composition.
This is the primary market gap.

MARKET OPPORTUNITY QUANTIFIED:

Total Addressable Market (TAM):
  • Global music composition industry: $5-10 billion
  • Custom composition segment: ~$200-500 million annually

Serviceable Market (SAM):
  • US-only custom scoring for creators: $50-100 million annually

Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):
  • Individual composer realistic capture: $5-50 million (if scaled to small agency)
  • Year 1-3 realistic: $100,000-500,000

STRATEGIC POSITIONING:

Do NOT compete on:
  ✗ Catalog size (Epidemic has 30K tracks, AudioJungle has 11M)
  ✗ Price (subscription commoditization race)
  ✗ Scale (they have VC funding, servers, scale)

DO compete on:
  ✓ Custom work (nobody else does this at scale)
  ✓ Personal relationships (they're algorithmic)
  ✓ Turnaround speed (48 hours vs weeks/months)
  ✓ Specialization (niche expertise)
  ✓ Quality (not volume)
  ✓ Direct client relationships (no middleman)

PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS:

Stream 1: Direct Composition Work (50-60% of revenue)
  • Per-project pricing: $600-50,000 depending on scope
  • Average project: $1,500-3,000
  • Volume: Start at 5-10 projects/month, scale to 30-50/month

Stream 2: Licensing Deals (30-40% of revenue)
  • Per-license pricing: $500-5,000 (depends on exclusivity, territory)
  • Volume: Start at 10-20/month, scale to 50-100/month
  • Passive: Once licensed, continues generating royalties
  • Example: 20 licenses × $750 avg = $15,000/month

Stream 3: Retainer Clients (10-20% of revenue)
  • Monthly retainer: $1,000-5,000/month per client
  • 2-3 pieces per month included
  • Volume: Start with 2-3 clients, scale to 10-15 clients
  • Example: 5 retainer clients × $2,000 = $10,000/month

Stream 4: Passive/Catalog Income (5-10%, grows over time)
  • Streaming royalties (Spotify, Apple Music)
  • Performance royalties (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC broadcast)
  • Future licensing from old projects
  • Example: Year 1-3 is minimal, Year 5 could be $5,000-10,000/month

  • Licensing: 80-90% margin (minimal delivery cost)

SECTION 8: RISK ANALYSIS & MITIGATION

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RISK 1: IP Infringement Claims

Severity: HIGH
Probability: LOW-MEDIUM
Impact: Legal fees ($5,000-50,000+), potential damages, reputation damage

Mitigation:
  ✓ Use only properly licensed samples/materials
  ✓ Document all materials' sources and licenses
  ✓ Get third-party IP review of questionable compositions
  ✓ Maintain Errors & Omissions insurance ($1M+)
  ✓ Have legal hold on client work until paid in full

---

RISK 2: Payment Default

Severity: MEDIUM
Probability: MEDIUM (10-20% of clients)
Impact: Unpaid invoices ($1,000-5,000 per incident)

Mitigation:
  ✓ 50% non-refundable deposit before starting work
  ✓ Milestone-based payment for large projects
  ✓ Invoice immediately upon completion
  ✓ Late payment penalties in contract (1.5%/month interest)
  ✓ Small claims court or collection agency as escalation
  ✓ Credit card payments (more enforcement options than bank transfers)

---

RISK 3: Scope Creep

Severity: MEDIUM
Probability: MEDIUM-HIGH (30-40% of projects)
Impact: Unprofitable projects, burnout, delayed delivery

Mitigation:
  ✓ Detailed SOW specifying exact deliverables
  ✓ Revision limits in contract (e.g., 2 rounds included)
  ✓ Out-of-scope work charged separately at $X/revision
  ✓ Change orders required for scope changes
  ✓ Client sign-off at each milestone
  ✓ Project tracking system (monitor hours vs estimate)

---

RISK 4: Market Saturation

Severity: MEDIUM
Probability: LOW-MEDIUM (2-3 year horizon)
Impact: Decreased pricing power, higher CAC to acquire customers

Mitigation:
  ✓ Specialize in specific niches (podcasts, gaming, etc.)
  ✓ Build reputation for excellence and speed
  ✓ Develop brand/personal following
  ✓ Referral partnerships create switching costs
  ✓ Retainer clients = recurring revenue base
  ✓ Build licensing catalog for passive income

---

RISK 5: Technology/Infrastructure Failure

Severity: MEDIUM
Probability: LOW (1-2% annually)
Impact: Data loss, project delays, reputation damage

Mitigation:
  ✓ Regular backups (daily to external drive + cloud)
  ✓ Version control for all projects
  ✓ Redundant DAW setup (laptop + desktop)
  ✓ Cloud storage with sync (Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive)
  ✓ Incident response plan documented
  ✓ Communication templates for outages

---

RISK 6: Burnout/Overwork

Severity: MEDIUM
Probability: MEDIUM-HIGH (Year 2-3 if not managed)
Impact: Quality decline, missed deadlines, health issues

Mitigation:
  ✓ Set clear project limits (e.g., max 5 projects/week)
  ✓ Build team/outsource overflow work at Year 2-3
  ✓ Establish project scheduling system (prevents overbooking)
  ✓ Establish business hours (stop work at defined time)
  ✓ Regular breaks/vacation time
  ✓ Monitor revenue per hour (ensures sustainable pricing)

---

RISK 7: Competitive Response

Severity: LOW-MEDIUM
Probability: MEDIUM (Year 3+ when model proven successful)
Impact: Price pressure, market dilution

Mitigation:
  ✓ Build personal brand + reputation (defensible)
  ✓ Establish referral partnerships (switching costs)
  ✓ Specialize in specific niches
  ✓ Build retainer client base (recurring revenue)
  ✓ Consider corporate offering (white-label for platforms)
  ✓ Continuous innovation (new services, better processes)
