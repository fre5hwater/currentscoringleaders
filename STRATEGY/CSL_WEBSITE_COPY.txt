[FAQ SECTION]

Q: What's included in the price?
A: Deliverables, revision structure, formats, and rights are defined clearly
   before work begins so the process stays professional and predictable.

Q: How does payment work?
A: Most projects begin with a 50% deposit and conclude with the remaining 50%
   on final delivery.

Q: Do I own the music?
A: Ownership depends on the selected rights model: work-for-hire, exclusive
   license, or non-exclusive license.

Q: What affects the quote most?
A: Duration, complexity, rights, deliverables, revision scope, and timeline.

Q: Do you offer rush delivery?
A: Yes, when schedule and scope allow. Rush work is quoted accordingly.

 [ABOUT SECTION — Founder story + philosophy]
 
 HEADING: About Current Scoring Leaders
 
 Current Scoring Leaders is a Pennsylvania-based custom scoring business founded
 by Avery Jones for creators, studios, and brands that want music built around
 actual identity, not generic convenience.
 
 The business exists because modern content has more music available than ever,
 but distinct sonic identity is harder to find than ever. Libraries and AI tools
 can provide speed, but they do not replace intentional direction, emotional
 precision, or music shaped for a real project.
 
 CSL is built around a simple idea:
 the music should strengthen the work, not just sit behind it.
 
 Whether the need is a signature creator theme, a branded podcast sound,
 story-aware scoring for visual media, or immersive music for a game world,
 the goal stays the same: build something specific enough to be remembered.
