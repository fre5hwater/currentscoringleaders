        CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — COMPETITOR QUALITY BENCHMARK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE

This file benchmarks the quality level CSL should match or exceed on the public
website.

Reference patterns reviewed from:
- working composer sites with stronger proof and buyer flow
- pricing/commission education sources
- game composer and freelance composer positioning examples

The goal is not to copy another site.
The goal is to understand what makes stronger sites feel more premium,
trustworthy, and conversion-ready.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. WHAT STRONGER SITES DO WELL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A. THEY SELL OUTCOMES, NOT JUST MUSIC

Stronger sites do not just say:
- I compose music

They say things like:
- help your game feel more immersive
- help your trailer impress publishers
- help your brand sound recognizable
- help your show feel memorable

CSL implication:
The site should present music as a business/creative advantage, not just an
art service.


B. THEY USE NAMED PROOF

Stronger sites show:
- named testimonials
- project names
- review quotes
- credits
- case studies
- sometimes press or player quotes

CSL implication:
A site with only generic service copy will feel weaker than competitors.
Proof must become a major design layer.


C. THEY SHOW A CLEAR WORKING RELATIONSHIP

Better sites explain:
- how feedback works
- how drafts are shared
- how deadlines are managed
- how communication stays organized

CSL implication:
Buyers need to feel the process is professional before they inquire.
This reduces fear and increases trust.


D. THEY MAKE CONTACT EASY

Stronger sites use simple, repeated CTAs like:
- contact me now
- work with me
- get started
- request a quote

CSL implication:
The site should avoid CTA confusion.
There should be one clear project-start path with lower-friction secondary
actions.


E. THEY EXPLAIN WHAT DRIVES PRICE

Pricing references consistently reinforce that cost changes based on:
- scope
- total minutes of music
- complexity/style
- revisions
- rights/ownership
- deadline speed
- deliverables

CSL implication:
The public website should show starting points and also explain why custom
quotes vary.
That makes the pricing feel more professional and less arbitrary.


F. THEY FEEL LIKE A REAL BUSINESS, NOT A HOBBY PAGE

Stronger sites communicate:
- professionalism
- deadlines
- structure
- business terms
- confidence
- specificity

CSL implication:
The website needs stronger page hierarchy, clearer sections, cleaner pricing,
rights clarity, and trust language.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. WHAT CURRENT CSL MATERIALS ALREADY DO WELL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Current strengths:
- strong anti-generic positioning
- clear custom vs stock argument
- buyer-first onboarding documents now exist
- legal pages and service contract already exist
- pricing starting points already exist
- FAQ/trust file already addresses common objections
- outreach and offer positioning are clearer than before

These are solid foundations.
The remaining issue is presentation quality and proof depth.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. WHERE CSL STILL FEELS WEAKER THAN TOP COMPETITOR-STYLED SITES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PROOF IS TOO THIN
- portfolio placeholders exist
- stronger named testimonials/case studies are missing from public flow

2. PRICING PRESENTATION IS FUNCTIONAL BUT NOT PREMIUM
- starting points exist
- buyer-friendly comparison logic is still too basic
- add-ons, rights options, and quote drivers should be shown more clearly

3. BUYER FLOW NEEDS A PREMIUM STRUCTURE
- inquiry path exists
- project-start page exists
- but the site needs a more obvious progression:
  hear work -> understand fit -> see pricing -> trust process -> inquire

4. ABOUT PAGE NEEDS AUTHORITY, NOT JUST DESCRIPTION
- the business story exists
- but it needs a stronger founder point of view, philosophy, and working style

5. VISUAL DIRECTION IS UNDER-SPECIFIED
- current notes are minimal
- premium sites use more intentional layout hierarchy, contrast, motion, and
  proof modules

6. LEGAL/TRUST PAGES MAY BE TOO DRY IF USED RAW
- legal content exists
- but website visitors need human-readable summaries first


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. PREMIUM WEBSITE ELEMENTS CSL SHOULD ADD OR STRENGTHEN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Must-have public elements:
- featured work section by use case
- case-study style proof blocks
- testimonials with names/project context when available
- premium pricing comparison section
- clear "what affects price" block
- deliverables/included items block
- rights and ownership explainer block
- process timeline with response expectations
- what happens after inquiry section
- founder/authority/about section with stronger voice
- FAQ preview on key pages
- project-start page with friction-reducing clarity

Nice-to-have premium elements:
- featured logos/credits strip
- before/after music examples
- short reel/video header
- embedded review quotes
- downloadable one-page services guide or rate guide


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. DESIGN QUALITY RULES FOR CSL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The final site should feel:
- premium
- cinematic
- modern
- structured
- easy to scan
- easy to trust
- easy to contact

The site should not feel:
- generic
- thin
- template-ish
- crowded
- overhyped
- vague
- visually cheap


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. CONCLUSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CSL already has enough strategic content to compete.
What it needs now is a stronger premium public presentation layer:
- more proof
- more hierarchy
- better buyer guidance
- better visual direction
- clearer pricing logic
- stronger trust framing

That is what the next website upgrade files should solve.
