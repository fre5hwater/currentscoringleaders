1.1 BUSINESS ENTITY STRUCTURE

Recommended: LLC (Limited Liability Company)

Formation Requirements:
  • Articles of Organization (file with state Secretary of State)
  • EIN Application (IRS Form SS-4) - $0 cost, instant online
  • Business license registration with state/county ($50-500 depending on state)
  • DBA registration if operating under different name ($25-100)
  • Business bank account (open with Federal EIN)

Liability Protection Scope:
  ✓ Protects personal assets from business debts
  ✓ Separates personal & business liability
  ✓ Limited protection if LLC acts fraudulently
  ✓ Does NOT protect personal tax obligations
  ✓ Insurance is still critical (not substitute for contracts)

Recommended Insurance:
  • General Liability: $1M minimum (~$400-800/year)
  • Errors & Omissions: $1-2M ($500-1,000/year)
  • Cyber Liability: For digital assets ($300-500/year)
  • Professional Indemnity: For contract breaches
  • Total: ~$1,200-2,300/year

Tax Benefits of LLC:
  ✓ Pass-through taxation (avoid corporate double-tax)
  ✓ Can elect S-Corp for further tax optimization (accountant recommendation)
  ✓ Deductible business expenses (equipment, software, contractor payments)
  ✓ Home office deduction (~$1,680/year if 280 sq ft @ $6/sq ft)
  ✓ Quarterly estimated tax payments (no surprises at year-end)

1.2 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGISTRATION

Copyrights (CRITICAL - Do First):

Original Compositions:
  • Automatic upon creation (need not register, but highly recommended)
  • US Copyright Office registration: $65 per work (2024)
  • Benefits of registration:
    - Creates public record of ownership
    - Enables statutory damages ($150-$30,000 per work infringement)
    - Enables attorney fees recovery
    - Required for US infringement lawsuits
    - Register within 3 months of publication for maximum protection
  • Timeline: 2-6 weeks for registration
  • Recommendation: Register all compositions yearly in bulk ($65 each)

Work-for-Hire Agreements (CRITICAL - Always Use):
  • Must be in writing before work begins
  • Transfers ALL copyright to client
  • Protects you: Retain rights to processes, templates, pre-existing materials
  • Template provided in Legal Framework document
  • Example language: "All original compositions created under this agreement 
    shall be works made for hire owned entirely by Client."
  • Saves disputes: Clear ownership = no litigation

Licensing Strategy:
  • Register with performing rights organization (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC)
  • Enables collection of performance royalties (broadcast, streaming)
  • Annual fees: $50-300 (one-time or annual)
  • Passive income: $100-5,000+/month if catalog grows and gets used
  • Register separately: Publishing rights vs writer royalties

Trademarks (Brand Protection):
  • Service mark registration: $250-400 per mark (federal)
  • Protects brand name/logo from competitors
  • Timeline: 4-9 months for federal registration
  • Renewal: Every 10 years
  • Domain names: Register all key variants (.com, .io, .net, etc.)

1.6 RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Risk 1: IP Infringement Claims
  Severity: HIGH | Probability: LOW-MEDIUM | Impact: $5K-50K+ legal fees

  Prevention:
    ✓ Use only properly licensed samples/loops
    ✓ Document all materials' sources
    ✓ Keep license terms on file
    ✓ Validate third-party licenses permit your use
    ✓ Get third-party IP review of questionable pieces

  Detection:
    ✓ Use Shazam/Soundhound for accidental similarity
    ✓ Search Copyright Office records
    ✓ Review compositions for accidentally similar melodies

  Response:
    ✓ Do not admit liability
    ✓ Consult IP attorney immediately
    ✓ Review insurance coverage
    ✓ Notify insurance carrier within claim notice window (30 days)

Risk 2: Payment Default
  Severity: MEDIUM | Probability: MEDIUM (10-20%) | Impact: $1K-5K per incident

  Prevention:
    ✓ 50% non-refundable deposit before starting
    ✓ Milestone-based payment for large projects
    ✓ Invoice immediately upon completion
    ✓ Credit card payments (more enforcement than bank transfers)
    ✓ Check client creditworthiness (ask for references)

  Escalation Path:
    ✓ Day 15 late: Suspend project, send payment demand
    ✓ Day 30 late: Formal demand letter, 1.5%/month interest accrual
    ✓ Day 45 late: Small claims court or collection agency
    ✓ Attorney sends formal demand (powerful signal)

Risk 3: Scope Creep
  Severity: MEDIUM | Probability: MEDIUM-HIGH (30-40%) | Impact: Unprofitable work

  Prevention:
    ✓ Detailed SOW with exact deliverables (e.g., "3 compositions, 30-60 sec each")
    ✓ Revision limits clearly stated (e.g., "2 rounds included")
    ✓ Out-of-scope work priced separately ($X per round)
    ✓ Change orders required for any scope changes
    ✓ Client sign-off at each milestone

  Tracking:
    ✓ Log hours on open-ended projects
    ✓ Document scope change requests
    ✓ Get written confirmation before deviating
    ✓ Calculate profitability by project stage

7.2 AI PIVOTS AND INDUSTRY DIRECTION

Key verified trends (2024–2025):
  • Epidemic: Pushing AI-powered tools and plugins to speed up discovery and
    adaptation for creators; still library-first, not custom scoring.
  • Artlist: Repositioning as an end-to-end AI production ecosystem
    (video, templates, sound) built on a large subscription base.
  • Envato/AudioJungle: Doubling down on Elements-style subscriptions and
    marketplace scale rather than personalized services.

Implications for Current Scoring Leaders:
  • The "big three" are significantly larger than our original estimates,
    but they remain catalog/subscription products, not boutique scorers.
  • AI is accelerating commoditized, generic background music and simple
    sync needs (YouTube, TikTok, low-budget content).
  • As AI flattens the middle of the market, the ceiling for premium,
    truly custom narrative scoring with human relationships actually rises.
  • Clients burned out on "everyone using the same library tracks" will be
    primed for high-touch, bespoke services like CSL.
