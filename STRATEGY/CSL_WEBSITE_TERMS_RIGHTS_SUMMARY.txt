1. HOW RIGHTS ARE HANDLED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Current Scoring Leaders does not force one ownership model onto every project.
Rights are structured based on the use case and defined in writing before work
is finalized.

Common options include:
- work-for-hire
- exclusive license
- non-exclusive license

That means clients can choose the level of ownership or exclusivity that fits
what they actually need.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. WHAT IS USUALLY INCLUDED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Most projects include:
- agreed deliverables
- defined revision rounds
- standard delivery formats
- written scope
- written payment structure
- written rights structure

Nothing important should live in vague assumptions.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. PAYMENT STRUCTURE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Most projects begin with:
- 50% deposit to begin
- 50% due on final delivery

Alternative milestone structures can be used when the project is larger or more
complex.

Final deliverables are released after final payment is complete.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. REVISIONS SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Revisions are included and defined up front.

Included revisions typically cover:
- arrangement adjustments
- mix adjustments
- timing/length refinements
- moderate instrumentation changes

Major direction changes, major genre changes, or major re-composition requests
are treated as scope changes rather than assumed freebies.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. REVIEW WINDOWS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Clients are given a defined review window to provide feedback.
This helps keep projects moving and prevents the process from stretching
indefinitely.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. DELIVERY SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Standard delivery commonly includes:
- WAV
- MP3

Depending on the agreement, additional items may be included or added:
- stems
- alternate cuts
- MIDI
- multitracks


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. CANCELLATION SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before work begins:
- deposit is typically refundable

After work begins:
- deposit is typically non-refundable
- work completed and committed time are billable

Completed and delivered work is generally non-refundable.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. WEBSITE PRESENTATION RULE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The website should present this information in two layers:

Layer 1:
- clear, human-readable summary blocks

Layer 2:
- full contract, terms, privacy, and licensing documents

This keeps the site both professional and understandable.
