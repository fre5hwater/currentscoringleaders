         CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — ONBOARDING LANDING PAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE

This page is the buyer-entry page for people ready to begin a project.
It should qualify the buyer, build trust, explain the process, and move them
smoothly into the intake form and payment path.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. PAGE GOAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The page should answer:
- whether CSL is a fit
- what kinds of projects are accepted
- what the public starting points are
- how the process works
- what happens after submission
- when payment happens

Primary outcome:
- completed project intake

Secondary outcome:
- discovery call for higher-complexity buyers

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. PAYMENT / BILLABLE STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Recommended buyer path:
- landing page
- intake form
- quote or recommendation
- deposit payment page
- kickoff confirmation page

Recommended payment rule:
- 50% deposit to begin
- remaining balance before final delivery

For simple projects:
- allow faster direct quote and checkout

For larger projects:
- use scoped proposal first
