       CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — WEBSITE MEDIA + MISSING ASSETS LIST

PURPOSE

This file identifies the assets that would most improve the final website but
cannot be fully generated from text alone.

These are the assets Avery should create, gather, or approve if CSL wants the
site to feel fully premium rather than just well-written.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. HIGHEST-VALUE MEDIA ASSETS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. HERO REEL / SHORT BRAND VIDEO
What it is:
- a 20 to 45 second brand reel with strong visuals and CSL music

Why it matters:
- instantly raises perceived quality
- makes the site feel alive and premium
- proves the music against real media

What to include:
- clips of video work, editing, podcast visuals, game footage, or motion scenes
- paired with a strong CSL piece
- ending with Current Scoring Leaders branding


2. FOUNDER / STUDIO PORTRAIT ASSETS
What it is:
- clean portraits or studio workspace images of Avery / CSL environment

Why it matters:
- makes the business feel real and trustworthy
- strengthens the About page
- humanizes the buying decision


3. FEATURED AUDIO EXAMPLES WITH PROJECT CONTEXT
What it is:
- final selected tracks with titles, use cases, durations, and short notes

Why it matters:
- the portfolio cannot stay placeholder-based
- public proof needs context, not just audio files


4. TESTIMONIALS / PROOF
What it is:
- real client or collaborator testimonials
- names, projects, roles, and permission to publish

Why it matters:
- named proof is one of the biggest quality upgrades a site can get

If real client testimonials are limited:
- use honest demo/project context
- use collaborator feedback
- use public review language only if permission/use is appropriate


5. PROJECT VISUALS / MOCKUPS
What it is:
- stills, thumbnails, episode artwork, game shots, creator branding visuals,
  or mockups paired with music entries

Why it matters:
- makes portfolio entries feel like real applications
- helps visitors understand what the music is for
