         CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — PORTFOLIO CARD TEMPLATE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE

Use this template for:
- demo concepts
- spec pieces
- real client portfolio entries
- outreach proof examples

The goal is to make every portfolio item easy to understand quickly.
A prospect should immediately see:
- what kind of project it fits
- what the music was solving
- why it matters
- what to do next

6. RULES FOR USING THIS TEMPLATE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Keep the brief short and specific.
- Do not oversell the result.
- Do not fake client context.
- Do use labels honestly.
- Do make the use case obvious.
- Do match the CTA to the offer.
