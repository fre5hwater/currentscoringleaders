1. CORE POSITIONING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Current Scoring Leaders is NOT a stock library.
Current Scoring Leaders is NOT generic AI background music.
Current Scoring Leaders is a premium custom scoring service for creators,
studios, and brands that need music built specifically for their story,
identity, and audience.

Core positioning line:
"Custom scoring for creators who have outgrown generic library tracks."

Supporting angles:
- Story-first, not track-first
- Relationship-driven, not marketplace-driven
- Human-directed, not prompt-dumped
- Tailored to the project, not recycled from a catalog
- Premium but still accessible compared to high-end studio composer rates

Why this matters now:
- Epidemic, Artlist, and Envato are bigger than originally estimated
- All are leaning harder into scale, subscriptions, and AI tools
- None of them solve the "make my brand/project sound uniquely mine" problem
- AI makes generic music cheaper, which increases the value of distinct music

2. PRIMARY SEGMENTS TO TARGET FIRST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SEGMENT A — YOUTUBE CREATORS / VIDEO CREATORS

Best fit because:
- Fastest outreach volume
- Easy to find leads publicly
- Clear pain point: repetitive stock music
- Strong repeat business potential

Pain points:
- Their channel sounds like everyone else
- Their intro lacks sonic identity
- Stock tracks create brand sameness
- Music choices often weaken retention and emotional punch

Best offers:
- Signature Theme Package
- Episode / Video Scoring
- Monthly Creator Retainer


SEGMENT B — PODCASTERS / PODCAST NETWORKS

Best fit because:
- Direct response to custom intro/outro value
- Branding matters heavily
- Strong recurring refresh opportunities

Pain points:
- Show intro/outro sounds generic
- No recognizable sonic branding
- Hard to stand out in crowded podcast space

Best offers:
- Podcast Theme Package
- Show Refresh Package
- Monthly Audio Identity Retainer


SEGMENT C — INDIE GAME STUDIOS

Best fit because:
- Clear need for world-specific music
- High emotional value of custom score
- Potential for larger project sizes

Pain points:
- Stock music breaks immersion
- Need consistent tone across scenes/areas
- Want loops, stems, and adaptive structure

Best offers:
- Game / Narrative Pack
- Character + Location Theme Package
- Milestone-based soundtrack package


SEGMENT D — SMALL PRODUCTION HOUSES / EDITING AGENCIES

Best fit because:
- Referral leverage
- Better budgets than hobby creators
- Ongoing project flow possible

Pain points:
- Need reliable custom scorer on deadlines
- Stock tracks limit originality for clients
- Composer coordination is often slow or inconsistent

Best offers:
- Project-based custom scoring
- Monthly retainer
- Referral partner structure

Reliable custom scoring without re-hiring each time.

4. OUTREACH SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DAILY TARGET

- 20 to 30 new highly targeted contacts
- 5 to 10 follow-ups
- 5 to 10 CRM updates

WEEKLY TARGET

- 100 to 150 new contacts
- 5 to 10 warm conversations
- 2 to 5 calls booked
- 1 to 2 quotes sent

OUTREACH CHANNEL PRIORITY

1. Email
2. Instagram DM
3. YouTube / creator business contact forms
4. Discord / indie game communities (carefully)
5. Referral partner outreach

LEAD SOURCES

YouTubers:
- Search mid-size channels in defined niches
- Look for channels with strong editing but generic music
- Prioritize consistent uploaders

Podcasters:
- Search Apple Podcasts / Spotify charts by niche
- Prioritize shows with active audience and branding focus

Indie games:
- itch.io
- Steam upcoming / recent releases
- Indie dev Discord communities

Agencies / studios:
- Clutch
- agency websites
- Instagram reels editors / post houses
- LinkedIn company pages

LEAD QUALIFICATION FIELDS

Track for each lead:
- Name
- Company / channel / show
- Segment
- Niche
- Why they fit
- Current music quality
- Best offer
- Budget guess (low / medium / high)
- Contacted date
- Follow-up dates
- Status

5. MESSAGING ANGLES BY SEGMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ANGLE A — "YOU SOUND LIKE EVERYONE ELSE"

Best for:
- YouTubers
- Podcasters

Use when:
- Content is strong
- Music is clearly stock/generic

Main idea:
The visuals and editing are unique, but the sound identity is generic.


ANGLE B — "YOUR WORLD DESERVES ITS OWN SOUND"

Best for:
- Indie games
- narrative creators

Use when:
- The project has visible creative ambition
- Atmosphere matters heavily

Main idea:
A custom world needs custom sonic architecture.


ANGLE C — "RELIABLE CUSTOM MUSIC WITHOUT THE CHAOS"

Best for:
- Agencies
- production houses

Use when:
- They need dependable execution
- They likely juggle multiple deadlines

Main idea:
You provide custom scoring with clearer process and less friction.


ANGLE D — "ANTI-GENERIC / STORY-FIRST"

Best for:
- Higher-end prospects
- cinematic creators
- people tired of templated results

Main idea:
AI and stock can fill silence, but they cannot create identity.

6. CALL-TO-ACTION FRAMEWORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Preferred CTA hierarchy:

1. Free sample concept / theme direction
2. 15-minute discovery call
3. Reply with keyword (SAMPLE / INTRO / SOUNDTRACK / PARTNER)
4. Send project link for quick feedback

Why these CTAs work:
- Low friction
- Easy to answer quickly
- Concrete next step
- Not overly salesy

7. FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FOLLOW-UP 1 — Day 3 to 4

Goal:
- Simple inbox bump
- Re-state one specific benefit

FOLLOW-UP 2 — Day 7 to 8

Goal:
- Offer lighter-friction entry point
- Sample, short call, or smaller trial package

FOLLOW-UP 3 — Day 14

Goal:
- Close loop respectfully
- Leave door open
- Optionally move to long-term nurture list

Do NOT:
- Spam daily
- Send long walls of text
- Pitch all offers at once

9. SHARED LOGIC BETWEEN OUTREACH AND WEBSITE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Outreach and website must stay synchronized.

If outreach says:
- anti-generic
- story-first
- custom scoring
- premium but accessible

Then website must reinforce:
- anti-generic positioning
- clear offers
- professional process
- confidence and legitimacy

If a segment responds well in outreach:
- its language should influence homepage and service copy
- its proof points should become case studies or examples
- its objections should become FAQ content

If the website gets questions repeatedly:
- those objections should be pre-handled in outreach copy

Tracking rule:
- track exact time when possible
- track the strategy lane being used
- track the offer/message/CTA path being used
- track the LLM, model, and tools that supported the work
- track repeated objections, tendencies, and trend signals
- do not only track outcomes; track the conditions surrounding outcomes
