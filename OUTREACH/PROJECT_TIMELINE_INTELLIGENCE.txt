PRODUCTION TIMELINE ANALYSIS:
Understanding when music decisions are made is critical for strategic submission timing.
Each media type has different production cycles and music lock deadlines.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TV SERIES PRODUCTION CYCLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STANDARD TV SERIES TIMELINE (Episode-based):

PRE-PRODUCTION PHASE (3-6 months before air date):
├─ Script development & music concept discussions
├─ Budget allocation for music (original vs licensed)
└─ Music supervisor engagement

POST-PRODUCTION PHASE (2-4 months before air date):
├─ Week 12-16: Temp scoring begins
├─ Week 8-12: Original composition period
├─ Week 4-8: Music editing & integration
├─ Week 2-4: MUSIC LOCK DEADLINE ⚠️
├─ Week 1-2: Final audio mix
└─ Week 0: Episode delivery

KEY MUSIC SUBMISSION WINDOWS:
🔴 URGENT WINDOW (2-4 weeks before air date):
   - Final music decisions being made
   - Last-minute cue replacements
   - Emergency custom composition needs

🟡 STRATEGIC WINDOW (4-8 weeks before air date):
   - Custom composition requests
   - Catalog review for specific scenes
   - Music supervisor actively seeking tracks

🟢 PLANNING WINDOW (8-12 weeks before air date):
   - Catalog review and consideration
   - Building relationships for future episodes
   - Understanding show's music direction

SEASONAL PRODUCTION PATTERNS:
- Fall season (Sept-Nov): Post-production June-Aug
- Mid-season (Jan-Mar): Post-production Oct-Dec
- Summer season (May-Aug): Post-production Feb-Apr

FEATURE FILM PRODUCTION CYCLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FEATURE FILM TIMELINE (4-8 months before release):

POST-PRODUCTION PHASE:
├─ Month 6-8: Temp scoring & music concept
├─ Month 4-6: Original composition period
├─ Month 3-4: Music editing & orchestration
├─ Month 2-3: MUSIC LOCK DEADLINE ⚠️
├─ Month 1-2: Final audio mix & dubbing
└─ Month 0: Theatrical release

KEY MUSIC SUBMISSION WINDOWS:
🔴 URGENT WINDOW (2-3 months before release):
   - Final music decisions
   - Last-minute cue needs
   - Emergency replacements

🟡 STRATEGIC WINDOW (3-6 months before release):
   - Custom composition requests
   - Catalog review for specific scenes
   - Director/music supervisor collaboration

🟢 PLANNING WINDOW (6-8 months before release):
   - Early catalog consideration
   - Relationship building
   - Understanding film's musical direction

RELEASE PATTERN INTELLIGENCE:
- Summer blockbusters (May-Aug): Post-production Jan-Apr
- Awards season (Oct-Dec): Post-production Jun-Sep
- Holiday releases (Nov-Dec): Post-production Jul-Oct

DEVELOPMENT PHASE:
