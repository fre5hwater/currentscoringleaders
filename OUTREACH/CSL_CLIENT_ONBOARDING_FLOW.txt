          CURRENT SCORING LEADERS — CLIENT ONBOARDING FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PURPOSE

This document defines a buyer-first onboarding flow for Current Scoring Leaders.
The goal is to make first contact feel professional, clear, low-friction, and
worth paying for.

This flow should reduce:
- uncertainty
- back-and-forth confusion
- pricing hesitation
- vague project requests
- dropped leads between interest and payment


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. BUYER-FIRST GOALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The client should immediately understand:
- what CSL does
- whether they are a fit
- what happens next
- what information is needed
- when money is due
- what they receive after paying

The process should feel:
- premium
- simple
- organized
- trustworthy
- human


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. RECOMMENDED ONBOARDING FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1 — LANDING PAGE / INQUIRY ENTRY
Purpose:
- qualify the lead before they ever contact CSL

What the page should do:
- explain who this is for
- explain what CSL offers
- show public starting points
- explain process simply
- direct buyer to one primary CTA

Primary CTA:
- Start Your Project

Secondary CTA:
- Ask a Question


STEP 2 — PROJECT INTAKE FORM
Purpose:
- collect the right info once
- reduce long email ping-pong

What happens here:
- client fills project brief
- client selects project type
- client shares timeline and budget range
- client uploads references or links

Best framing:
This is not busywork.
This is how CSL recommends the right path quickly.


STEP 3 — INTERNAL REVIEW
Purpose:
- determine fit
- estimate scope
- choose quote path

Possible outcomes:
- fit for direct quote
- fit for discovery call
- fit for paid strategy / scoring consult
- not a fit


STEP 4 — RESPONSE PATH
Purpose:
- give the buyer a clear next step instead of ambiguity

Recommended response options:
- direct quote if scope is clear
- discovery call link if project needs discussion
- recommendation email with best package starting point
- polite decline if outside fit


STEP 5 — PROPOSAL / OFFER PAGE
Purpose:
- confirm scope and price
- reduce confusion before payment

This should include:
- project summary
- deliverables
- timeline
- revision structure
- rights model
- payment terms
- deposit amount
- next step button


STEP 6 — DEPOSIT / PAYMENT PAGE
Purpose:
- convert approved buyer into active client

Recommended structure:
- 50% deposit payment link
- invoice or checkout summary
- contract acknowledgment
- confirmation after payment

Strong buyer experience rule:
Do not make payment feel disconnected from the plan.
The buyer should clearly know what they are paying for.


STEP 7 — CLIENT WELCOME / KICKOFF PAGE
Purpose:
- reinforce professionalism after purchase
- lower buyer anxiety immediately

What happens here:
- confirm payment received
- confirm timeline
- explain what CSL needs next
- explain when first draft or next step arrives
- provide contact path


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. BEST STRUCTURE FOR DIFFERENT BUYER TYPES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FAST-MOVING BUYERS
Examples:
- creators
- podcasters
- smaller custom-theme clients

Best path:
- landing page
- intake form
- direct recommendation or quote
- deposit
- kickoff


HIGHER-COMPLEXITY BUYERS
Examples:
- agencies
- production companies
- indie game projects

Best path:
- landing page
- intake form
- discovery call or scoped review
- proposal page
- deposit
- kickoff


REFERRAL PARTNER BUYERS
Examples:
- editor referred a client
- agency intro

Best path:
- short landing page
- simplified intake
- direct human follow-up
- proposal / deposit


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. PAYMENT STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Recommended standard:
- 50% deposit to begin
- 50% before final delivery

For larger or longer projects:
- milestone payments

For simpler theme packages:
- one-page checkout tied to package summary

For retainers:
- application
- approval
- recurring payment setup

Buyer trust rule:
Never ask for payment before the project path feels clear.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. WHAT THE LANDING PAGE SHOULD ANSWER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The buyer needs answers to:
- Is this for a project like mine?
- What do you actually offer?
- What do projects start at?
- How do I begin?
- Do I need a call first?
- What happens after I fill this out?
- When do I pay?
- What rights do I receive?


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. WHAT THE FORM SHOULD COLLECT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Required:
- name
- email
- company or project name
- project type
- project description
- timeline
- budget range

Helpful:
- reference links
- current content link
- desired tone
- deliverable needs
- whether licensing or full ownership is needed
- whether rush timing exists


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER FORM SUBMISSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Recommended confirmation message:
- confirm receipt
- explain review timing
- explain likely next step
- reassure them they will receive a human response

Best expectation:
- reply within 24 hours


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. BUYER EXPERIENCE WARNINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Avoid:
- too many CTAs
- unclear package differences
- payment before trust is built
- vague turnaround promises
- giant forms up front without context
- making simple buyers book calls unnecessarily


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9. RECOMMENDED BUILD ORDER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. onboarding landing page
2. intake form
3. confirmation page
4. proposal template
5. deposit / payment page
6. client welcome page
