Creative Process

From brief to delivery. Every project follows the same rigorous path.

01

Discovery

We learn your project, its story, and its emotional targets. What does this music need to say?

02

Direction

Creative brief with references, stylistic direction, and structural outline. You approve before we write a note.

03

First Draft

Full composition delivered within 48 hours. You hear the complete vision, not a sketch.

04

Refinement

Two rounds of revisions included. Structural, tonal, and mix adjustments. Your vision, perfected.

05

Delivery

Mastered WAV + MP3. Signed licensing agreement. Full ownership or license terms as agreed.
