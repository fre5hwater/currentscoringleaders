6. DELIVERY AND REVISIONS

Standard Turnaround:
  - Most projects: 5-10 business days
  - Rush orders: 48 hours (additional fee applies)
  - Complex/premium projects: Custom timeline per SOW

Revisions Included:
  - Starter Package: 2 rounds of revisions
  - Standard Package: 3 rounds of revisions
  - Premium Package: Unlimited revisions during project period
  - Additional revision rounds available at agreed hourly rate

Revision Scope:
  - Included: Arrangement changes, mix adjustments, length modifications,
    moderate instrumentation changes
  - Not included: Complete re-composition, major genre/style changes,
    significant scope expansion (requires change order)

Revision Process:
  1. Client provides written feedback within 7 days of draft delivery
  2. CSL completes revisions within agreed timeline
  3. Client reviews and approves or provides additional feedback
  4. Process repeats up to the revision limit

Acceptance:
  - If no feedback is received within 7 business days of delivery,
    the project is deemed accepted as delivered
  - No further revisions will be available after acceptance

Delivery Format:
  - Standard: WAV (44.1kHz/24-bit) + MP3 (320kbps)
  - With stems: Individual track stems in WAV format
  - Additional formats available upon request
