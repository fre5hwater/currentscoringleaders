  • Contractor: [X] days written notice

Company may terminate immediately for:

  • Work quality fails to meet standards
  • Failure to meet deadline (without approval)
  • Unauthorized disclosure of confidential info
  • Illegal conduct

9.3 Upon Termination

  ✓ All work created through termination owned by Company
  ✓ Work completed to date compensated (if not breach)
  ✓ All materials and files returned
  ✓ All confidential information deleted
  ✓ Confidentiality obligations survive

9.4 Severance

If terminated without cause:
  • Contractor paid for work completed to date
  • No severance or additional compensation
  • All work remains Company property

If terminated for cause:
  • Contractor paid only for acceptable work completed
  • Work in progress forfeited (no compensation)
