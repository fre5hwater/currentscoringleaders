1. INTRODUCTION

Current Scoring Leaders LLC ("CSL," "Company," "we," "us," "our") is
committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we
collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our
website, use our services, or interact with us.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website or
services. We may update this policy at any time; material changes will be
communicated via email to active clients with 30 days' notice.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information You Provide

When you contact us, request a quote, or engage our services, we collect:
  - Name and business name
  - Email address
  - Phone number
  - Mailing address (if applicable)
  - Project details and creative briefs
  - Payment information (processed securely by third-party processors)
  - Communication history (emails, messages, call notes)
  - Files or content you upload for project purposes

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:
  - IP address
  - Browser type and version
  - Operating system
  - Pages visited and time spent
  - Referring URL
  - Device type

This data is collected via cookies, analytics tools (Google Analytics),
and server log files.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive limited information from:
  - Payment processors (transaction status)
  - Analytics platforms (aggregated usage data)
  - Referral partners (referral source only)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  - Provide our services (composition, scoring, licensing)
  - Process payments and deliver invoices
  - Communicate about your project and deliverables
  - Respond to inquiries and support requests
  - Send project updates, confirmations, and receipts
  - Improve our website and services
  - Comply with legal and tax obligations
  - Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  - Send marketing communications (only with your consent)

We will NOT:
  - Sell your personal information to third parties
  - Share your information with competitors
  - Use your information for purposes not described here

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

4.1 Service Providers

We share necessary information with trusted third-party providers who
assist us with:
  - Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
  - Email delivery and communication
  - Analytics (Google Analytics)
  - Cloud storage and file delivery
  - Accounting and invoicing

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use
it only for the services they provide to us.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order,
subpoena, or government request.

4.3 Business Transfers

If CSL is acquired, merged, or undergoes reorganization, your information
may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of
any such change.

4.4 With Your Consent

We will only share your information with other parties when you
explicitly consent.

4.5 Aggregated Data

We may share anonymized, aggregated data (which cannot identify you
personally) for analytics or reporting purposes.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  - SSL/TLS encryption on all website pages (HTTPS)
  - Secure payment processing through PCI-compliant providers
  - Password-protected accounts and systems
  - Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  - Regular review of security practices

No system is 100% secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot
guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6. DATA RETENTION

  - Active client data: Retained during engagement and for 3 years after
    project completion
  - Project records and invoices: Retained for 7 years (tax/legal)
  - Marketing/email lists: Retained until you unsubscribe
  - Analytics data: Aggregated data retained; individual tracking data
    retained for 13 months then deleted
  - Backups: Maintained for 3 months minimum, then securely destroyed

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to
legal or contractual retention requirements. We will process deletion
requests within 30 days.

7. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  - Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  - Request correction of inaccurate information
  - Request deletion of your personal data
  - Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  - Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:
privacy@currentscoringleaders.com

We will respond to rights requests within 14 business days.

7.1 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:
  - Right to know what personal information is collected
  - Right to delete personal information
  - Right to opt out of the sale of personal information
  - Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

We do not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, email
privacy@currentscoringleaders.com.

7.2 EU/UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, your additional rights include:
  - Right to data portability
  - Right to restrict processing
  - Right to object to processing
  - Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Legal basis for processing: Contract performance, legitimate business
interests, legal obligations, and consent (where applicable).
